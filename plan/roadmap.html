Order of Battle

The prototype is built in 7 milestones. Each one ends with a version you can actually play, so you can steer early, not just at the end.

01

Phase 1: Prototype "Operation Breakfast"

ACCEPTANCE TESTS PASSED (ticked by you after playing)

Your ticks are saved in this browser only, as a personal tracker. For now, everything stays unticked: nothing has been built yet.

OUT OF SCOPE

Not in the prototype

  • Squadmates and squad commands
  • Weapons beyond rifle + grenade (bazooka pending Q4)
  • Gamepad support
  • Multiplayer of any kind
  • Save games, story, cutscenes, voice acting
  • Helicopters, base building, other levels
RISKS

What could slow us down

  • Vehicle feel: driving physics takes several rounds of your feedback to get right
  • Mixed asset styles: CC0 packs don't match each other. The plastic material is how we unify them.
  • Test loop: I can't play the game, so each round of fixes depends on your reports
  • Mac specifics: trackpad vs mouse aiming, and mouse capture behaviour on macOS. Test with a real mouse if you can.

After the prototype

PROPOSED · NOT YET APPROVED
PHASE 2

Vertical slice

Squad of 3 with simple orders, bazooka / flamethrower (melting!) / mortar / mines / sniper, a mounted jeep gun, gamepad support, rebindable keys, a full sound pass, and Tan tanks.

PHASE 3

Campaign

5–8 missions mixing real-world rooms and dioramas, a light story (Sarge vs General Plastro), saves, and a mission select screen.

PHASE 4

Air Attack

A flyable helicopter, air-support missions, and a co-pilot winch to pick up troops.

PHASE 5

Commander layer

RTS-lite: collect resources, build structures, give orders from a top-down tactical view, and drop into any soldier.

PHASE 6

Multiplayer?

Co-op or Green-vs-Tan PvP. A big piece of work, to be decided much later.

PHASE 6+

Toys in Space

A bonus episode with low gravity and space toys.

The Kitchen Front

The prototype map, the scale rules that make a kitchen feel enormous, and where the war goes after that.

01

Scale: how big is a kitchen to a 5 cm soldier?

The scaling rule

We keep the soldier at normal human size in the engine (1.8 m) and scale the kitchen up 36× instead. Physics engines are tuned for human-sized objects. If we shrank the soldier to 5 cm instead, jumps, grenades and vehicles would behave strangely.

  • Real kitchen ~5 m × 4 m → game floor ~180 m × 144 m
  • That's roughly the size of 3 football pitches, plenty of room for a jeep and tank
  • CC0 furniture and food models get scaled 36× and given high-resolution textures so they stay sharp up close

Future theaters (after the prototype)

PROPOSED

Backyard

Grass taller than you, a garden hose river, a sandbox desert, a sprinkler that knocks soldiers over.

Bathroom

A slippery tile arena, a bathtub naval battle, and toothpaste you can climb.

Kid's bedroom

A block-built fortress, a toy race track as a highway, enemy forces under the bed.

Garage

Paint-can towers, a toolbox bunker, and an oil-spill slick for vehicles.

Desert

Sand dunes and an oasis. Long sightlines suit snipers and tanks.

Jungle

Dense foliage, river crossings, ambushes.

Alpine snow

Mountain pass, ice-covered lakes, ski-mounted Tan troops.

Beach landing

A landing-craft assault on a fortified Tan beach.

PHASE 4

Air Attack

Helicopter missions over the backyard: fly a Huey, rescue squads, attack a Tan convoy.

PHASE 5

Commander ops

RTS-style missions: collect plastic, build a firebase, and give orders from an overhead view, then drop into any soldier.

PHASE 6+

Toys in Space

A bonus episode: low-gravity fights on a model moon base.

Note: these are only ideas to show the world can grow. None of them are in scope until the prototype is approved and playable.

Camera: third-person ⇄ first-person

Third-person (default)

  • Camera over the right shoulder, pulled back on a spring arm so it never clips through walls
  • Hold Right mouse to aim: the camera zooms in tighter and accuracy improves
  • Q swaps the camera to the other shoulder, useful when peeking around corners
  • You see your whole plastic soldier, which makes the toy look really stand out

First-person

  • Camera at eye level, with your arms and rifle visible
  • Hold Right mouse to aim down the sights
  • Same stats in both modes: switching view never makes you stronger or weaker
  • In vehicles: FPS puts you in the driver's seat or the tank commander's hatch

Switch: press V at any time PROPOSED KEY. The camera blends between the two positions in about 0.25 s instead of cutting.

Your last-used view is remembered separately for on-foot and vehicle play.

TAN · ENEMY

Tan rifleman

Health60
Accuracylow
Reaction0.6 s

Dies to about 3 body shots or 2 headshots. Their missed shots hit the cover near you so you can tell where fire is coming from.

COVER

Why cover matters

  • Standing in the open against 3+ Tan soldiers kills you in about 4 s
  • Crouching behind cover blocks about 80% of incoming fire
  • Grenades flush enemies out of cover. Tan soldiers throw them too.
  • Soft cover (cardboard, cereal boxes) breaks down. Hard cover (mugs, table legs) doesn't.

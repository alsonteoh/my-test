02

The four design pillars

Every feature has to support at least one of these. If it doesn't, it waits.

PILLAR 1

It's plastic

Soldiers look like toys: shiny molded plastic with faint mold seams. When killed they freeze into a stiff molded pose and topple over. Flamethrowers (later) melt them.

PILLAR 2

Your view, your call

Over-the-shoulder TPS for awareness, FPS for precise aim. Switch instantly with one key, on foot or in a vehicle.

PILLAR 3

Ride into battle

Jump in a jeep to cover ground fast, or take a tank to break through. Vehicles change the fight instead of just moving you around.

PILLAR 4

A giant world

At 5 cm tall, a kitchen is a war zone. Table legs are pillars, cereal boxes are bunkers, and the countertop is high ground.

04

The prototype in one card

Tan forces have built a radio post under the kitchen table. Fight across the kitchen floor, take the jeep for a fast flank, capture a tank, smash through the cereal-box wall and destroy the radio.

  • 1 playable green soldier with a rifle and grenades
  • Third-person ⇄ first-person toggle
  • Tan riflemen that patrol, take cover and flank PROPOSED: ~12–16 enemies
  • A drivable jeep and a drivable tank
  • 1 kitchen map (~180 m × 144 m in-game), a win/lose screen, and restart
  • Plastic look: glossy shader, toppling deaths, debris

The full layout is on the Theater page, and the step-by-step build is on the Roadmap page.
